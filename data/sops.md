## Police Systems

How the police script works in game. This section is accurate to the server, so keep it when you write the rest of the SOPs.

### Who counts as police
| Job name | Department | Plate prefix | Map blip colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| `police` | LSPD | LSPD | Blue |
| `bcso` | BCSO | BCSO | Green |
| `sahp` | SAHP | SAHP | Gold |

- EMS (`ambulance`) is not police, but on-duty EMS share your map blips and can use the panic button.
- Nearly everything below needs you to be **on duty**.

### Where things are (Mission Row PD)
| What | Where | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Duty point | Front lobby | Walk in, press **E** |
| Armory | Armory counter, main floor | Third eye > **Open Armory** |
| Evidence lockers | Evidence room, lower floor | Third eye > **Evidence Lockers** |
| Fingerprint scanner | Lower floor | Walk in, press **E** |
| Trash bin | Main floor | Walk in, press **E** (emptied on every restart) |
| Vehicle garage | Garage bay below the station | Walk onto the spot, press **E** |
| Impound lot | Next to MRPD, plus one at Paleto Bay | Walk in, press **E** |
| Helicopter | MRPD roof pad | Walk onto the pad, press **E** |
| Elevator | Lobby doors on each floor | Third eye > **Use Elevator** |

### Going on and off duty
- Stand at the duty point. You'll see **[E] - Go on duty** (or **[E] - Go off duty**). Press **E**.
- Your on-duty time is counted every minute and saved per week (weeks start Monday). Command staff can see it in `/training`.
- Clocking in resets your armory per-shift limits.
- Set your callsign with `/callsign [number]`. It has to be a number. It shows on your map blip, the panic alert and the armory screen.

### Map blips
- While on duty you see every on-duty LSPD, BCSO, SAHP and EMS member as `DEPT | callsign F. Lastname`.
- A unit's blip flashes when their sirens are on. Blips go away when you go off duty.

### Third eye on a player
Third eye a player within about 2 m (1.7 m for **Search**). You must be on duty and not cuffed or escorted yourself.

| Option | What it does | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **Search** | Opens their inventory | None |
| **Cuff / Uncuff** | Hard cuffs them, or uncuffs them if they're already cuffed | **Handcuffs** in your inventory, for cuffing *and* uncuffing. Neither of you can be in a vehicle |
| **Escort** | Grab them and walk them. Pick it again to let go | They must be cuffed, dead or downed |
| **Put in vehicle** | Puts them in a free seat of the nearest car, back seats first | They must be cuffed, dead or downed |
| **Check ID** | Shows their State ID for 12 s: name, DOB, gender, nationality, citizen ID, licenses (green = valid, red = revoked) | They get a notification |
| **GSR test** | 4 s swab. Result is **POSITIVE** or **NEGATIVE** | No kit needed. Positive if they fired a gun recently |
| **Breathalyzer** | 5 s test. Shows BAC, e.g. `0.112 BAC` | No item needed. The legal limit is **0.08** |
| **Seize items** | Opens the seize checklist (see below) | None |

To get someone out of a car, third eye the **vehicle** and choose **Pull person out**. Everyone in it who is cuffed, dead or downed gets pulled out.

### Seizing items (numbered evidence)
1. Third eye the suspect and choose **Seize items**.
2. You get a checklist titled "From <name>". Items on the illegal list are already ticked under **Illegal items**. Everything else is listed under **Everything else**, so you can tick it as evidence. Items inside their bags show as "In a bag".
3. Phones and cash never show up here. Use `/seizecash` for cash.
4. If you like, add a report note (case number or charge), then press **Seize N items**.
5. A card pops up: **Items Seized - Evidence #1042**. Every seizure gets its own locker, and numbers start at #1000. **Put the locker number in your report.**

### Evidence room
- Third eye the evidence spot and choose **Evidence Lockers**. You have to be on duty and standing there.
- **Search**: type a locker number (`1042` or `#1042`), a suspect name, an officer name, a word from the note, or a suspect's citizen ID. The list shows the 60 most recent matches.
- **Open**: opens that locker. Each locker is 50 slots.
- **New locker**: creates an empty locker by hand, for evidence found at a scene. Enter a note (case number, what it is) and it opens straight away as a new Evidence #.

### Armory
Third eye the counter and choose **Open Armory**. Everything is free, but the armory is locked by rank. Tabs: **Weapons, Ammo, Gear, Medical, Tools**. Each item shows what you carry out of the max, plus how many you have left this shift. Locked items show "Rank N+".

### Garage
- Stand on the garage spot while on foot. You'll see **[E] - Vehicle Garage**. Press **E**.
- You get a live preview of the car. Filter by category, drag to turn the car, step through **Livery**, toggle **Extra** buttons, and check top speed and seats. Then press **Take out <vehicle>**.
- You spawn in the driver's seat with keys, a full tank, and a plate like `LSPD1234`. The spot has to be clear, and you have to wait 5 s between spawns.
- To put a car away, drive onto the spot and press **E** (**[E] - Store Vehicle**).

### Helicopter
- On the MRPD roof pad, press **E** at **[E] Take Helicopter** to spawn a police Maverick (plate `ZULUxxxx`). Press **E** at **[E] Store Helicopter** to put it away.
- In the heli:

| Mouse wheel | Zoom |

| **X** | Rappel (rear seats, in the air) |

### Taser and cartridges
- A taser from the armory comes loaded with **2 shots**. Each shot uses one, and a bolt HUD shows what's loaded.
- When it's empty you can't fire. You'll see "Taser empty. Press R to reload."
- Press **R** with the taser out to reload. It takes 2.5 s and loads **Taser Cartridges** from your inventory, back up to 2.

### Panic button
- Press **Page Up** while on duty. EMS can use it too, and there's a 30 s cooldown.
- Every on-duty LEO and EMS gets a sound and a flashing red **PANIC** blip with an 80 m radius circle for 90 s. They also get a **10-99 Officer Needs Assistance** alert on the tablet, with your unit and street.
- You can rebind the taser and panic keys in Settings > Key Bindings > FiveM.

### Training (trainees and command)
- Officers at **grade 0 and 1** are trainees automatically. While they're on duty, illegal items show **highlighted in red** when they search someone.
- `/training` is for **command staff only (grades 7 to 10)**. It opens the **Training Roster** for your own department, with tabs **Everyone / In training / Online**. It shows each officer's rank, whether they're on duty or online, **Hours this week** and **Last week**.
- **Put in training** turns the red highlight back on for an officer without changing their rank. **Clear training** turns it off. You can only change officers ranked below you. Trainee-rank officers show "Promote to clear".
- Illegal list: joint, weed_skunk, cokebaggy, meth, heroin, lsd, coke/weed/meth bricks, thermite, markedbills, black_money, Micro SMG, Mini SMG, Machine Pistol, Assault Rifle, Compact Rifle, Sawn-off, Switchblade, Knuckles.

### Crime scene evidence
- To find evidence, go on duty, take out your **flashlight** and aim it. Within about 1.5 m you'll see **[G] Bullet Casing**, **[G] Blood** or **[G] Fingerprint**. Press **G** to bag it. Each one uses an **empty evidence bag**.
- `/clearcasings` and `/clearblood` clear everything within 10 m (5 s).
- `/takedna [id]` takes a DNA sample from that player. It needs an empty evidence bag.
- **Fingerprint scanner**: stand at the scanner and press **E**. The closest person (within 2.5 m) gets the scanner screen. When they press the pad, you both see their Fingerprint ID. **ESC** closes it.

| `/pobject [type]` | Places a prop: `cone`, `barrier`, `roadsign`, `tent`, `light`, `chair`, `chairs`, `tabe` (table), `monitor` |

### Impound lot
- On foot, press **E** at **[E] - Police Impound** to open **Impounded Vehicles**. It lists each car with its plate, engine and fuel. Pick one to release it and drive it out.
- In a car, **[E] - Impound Vehicle** at the lot only despawns that car. To actually impound one, use `/impound` or `/depot`.

### MRPD elevator
Third eye the elevator doors and choose **Use Elevator**. Then pick **Basement**, **Main Floor**, **Lounge** or **Helipad**. No floor is locked, so civilians can use it too.

### Good to know
- This script has **no MDT** and no `/bill` command. Speed cameras / ANPR are **turned off**, so plate flags only show up through `/plateinfo`.
